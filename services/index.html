01 Product lines

Four services. One ledger.

Each capability can be used alone. Together they form a complete payments and settlement stack.

Acceptance

Card, bank transfer and open-banking payment initiation through one integration. Tokenised order references mean you settle against a contract, not a correlation ID.

  • Hosted checkout
  • Server-to-server
  • 3-D Secure 2
  • Webhooks

Payouts

Supplier invoices, refunds and marketplace splits settled on a schedule you control. Every payout carries its own reconcilable trace.

  • Bulk CSV/API
  • Approval rules
  • Named-account check
  • Same-day FPS

Ledger & Reconciliation

Double-entry by default. Immutable, attributable entries with real-time trial balance. The month-end becomes a report, not an investigation.
