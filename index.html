02 The platform

One ledger, every direction of money.

Accept, hold, reconcile and disburse — without stitching together four providers and a spreadsheet.

/ acceptance

Accept payments

Card, bank transfer and open-banking initiation through a single integration. Tokenised references mean you settle against an order, not a guess.

  • Hosted checkout and server-to-server APIs
  • 3-D Secure 2 handled at the edge
  • Webhooks with idempotent delivery guarantees
/ payouts

Disburse with intent

Supplier invoices, refunds and marketplace splits settled on a schedule you control. Every payout carries its own reconcilable trace.

  • Bulk payouts via CSV or API
  • Rules-based approval thresholds
  • Named-account validation before funds leave
/ ledger

A ledger that closes

Double-entry by default. Each entry is immutable, attributable and queryable — so the month-end is a report, not an investigation.

  • Real-time balance and trial-balance views
  • Immutable audit trail per entry
  • Export to your existing accounting stack
