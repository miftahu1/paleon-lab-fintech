01 The company

A payments company run like a ledger.

Tessera Financial is a fictional UK business built for this test environment. The narrative below is illustrative only — there are no real customers, regulators or balances.

We started where the spreadsheets ended.

The fictional founding team came from payment operations, not venture capital. The story we tell is simple: money moves in many directions, but most tooling only handles one. Tessera exists to make the whole picture reconcilable.

In this lab the "company" is a shell. The interesting part is the externally observable infrastructure — the hosts, headers and services a passive scanner can see. That is what this environment is for.

02 What we value

Principles, not slogans.

Reconcilable by default

If a number cannot be traced to an entry, it is not a balance — it is a hope. We design for the audit, not around it.

Pricing you can read

A single per-transaction rate, published. No interchange mark-ups, no "call us for volume".

No lock-in

Your ledger is yours. Export everything, leave whenever you like, and bring your own bank.

British, boring, dependable

We would rather be the infrastructure nobody notices than the demo everyone forgets.

03 A note on this environment

This is a controlled test environment. Tessera Financial is not a real company. No financial services are offered, no real customer data exists, and no live payments are processed. The site is operated by Paleon purely to exercise a non-intrusive external scanner against a believable UK fintech surface.

If you arrived here expecting a product, you have found a lab. Everything — branding, statistics, API, hosts — is synthetic and safe to scan.
