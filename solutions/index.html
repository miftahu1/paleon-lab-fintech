01 Use cases

Patterns we see, patterns we solve.

Four common operating models. Each adds a different directional pressure on the ledger — we handle the ones that make controllers nervous.

/ marketplace

Marketplace & platform payouts

Collect from buyers, split to sellers, hold the float, remit on your schedule. Every split is a ledger entry — reconciliation is automatic.

  • Programmatic onboarding of sellers
  • Configurable hold periods per category
  • KYC/AML status surfaced in the console
  • 1099-K / DAC7 export helpers
/ subscription

Recurring revenue operations

Manage the full lifecycle — trial, active, paused, churned, reactivated. The ledger records every transition; dunning is a rule, not a spreadsheet.

  • Plan changes with proration entries
  • Automated retry cadences
  • Revenue recognition tags per entry
  • Churn attribution in the console
/ b2b

B2B invoice & credit management

Issue invoices, accept bank transfers, apply credits, reconcile to orders. The ledger knows the difference between "paid" and "applied".

  • Virtual IBAN per customer
  • Credit note and overpayment handling
  • Ageing report with drill-through
  • ERP posting templates
/ finance

Finance-team tooling

Trial balance, audit trail, export to Xero/QuickBooks/NetSuite. The same ledger your ops team uses, with the controls your controller expects.

  • Role-based access (viewer/approver/admin)
  • Immutable entry log with digital seal
  • Period-close checklist in the UI
  • CSV/JSON/IIF export on demand

02 Integration paths

How teams actually connect.

No-code: hosted checkout

Drop a link, send an invoice, embed a button. We host the payment page; you get the webhook.

Low-code: client SDK

React/Vue/vanilla components for card, bank pay and open banking. PCI SAQ-A eligible.

Full API: server-to-server

Complete control over the flow. Idempotent writes, deterministic sandbox, OpenAPI 3.1 spec.
